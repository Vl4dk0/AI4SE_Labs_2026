Examples (all text values in the database are lowercase; questions may contain irrelevant chatter that must be ignored):

Question: where can i get korean food in oakland ?
SQL: SELECT l.HOUSE_NUMBER, r.NAME FROM RESTAURANT r JOIN LOCATION l ON r.ID = l.RESTAURANT_ID WHERE r.CITY_NAME = 'oakland' AND r.FOOD_TYPE = 'korean';

Question: show me a place on college ave in berkeley with a rating above 2.5 (below average or better)?
SQL: SELECT l.HOUSE_NUMBER, r.NAME FROM RESTAURANT r JOIN LOCATION l ON r.ID = l.RESTAURANT_ID WHERE l.STREET_NAME = 'college ave' AND r.CITY_NAME = 'berkeley' AND r.RATING > 2.5;

Question: I keep wondering whether a kite can carry a sandwich, which cuban places in the bay area have a rating above 3 (good or better)?
SQL: SELECT l.HOUSE_NUMBER, r.NAME FROM RESTAURANT r JOIN LOCATION l ON r.ID = l.RESTAURANT_ID JOIN GEOGRAPHIC g ON r.CITY_NAME = g.CITY_NAME WHERE g.REGION = 'bay area' AND r.FOOD_TYPE = 'cuban' AND r.RATING > 3;

Question: which is the top rated thai spot in fremont ?
SQL: SELECT l.HOUSE_NUMBER, r.NAME FROM RESTAURANT r JOIN LOCATION l ON r.ID = l.RESTAURANT_ID WHERE r.CITY_NAME = 'fremont' AND r.FOOD_TYPE = 'thai' AND r.RATING = (SELECT MAX(r2.RATING) FROM RESTAURANT r2 WHERE r2.CITY_NAME = 'fremont' AND r2.FOOD_TYPE = 'thai');

Question: list some pizza places in the monterey region
SQL: SELECT l.HOUSE_NUMBER, r.NAME FROM RESTAURANT r JOIN LOCATION l ON r.ID = l.RESTAURANT_ID JOIN GEOGRAPHIC g ON r.CITY_NAME = g.CITY_NAME WHERE g.REGION = 'monterey' AND r.FOOD_TYPE = 'pizza';

Question: where is gallaghers pub in hayward ?
SQL: SELECT l.HOUSE_NUMBER, r.NAME FROM RESTAURANT r JOIN LOCATION l ON r.ID = l.RESTAURANT_ID WHERE r.NAME = 'gallaghers pub' AND r.CITY_NAME = 'hayward';
